Class 4: Capacitance & Prototyping

Explore charge storage, master timing loops via RC time constants, and learn the layout and safety rules of solderless breadboard building.

Syllabus Coverage
  • Capacitors: Electrostatic fields, step-by-step plate charging, and capacitance ($C = Q/V$)

  • Breadboard Lab: Solderless vertical row columns vs. horizontal power rails rules
  • Interactive step charging, plates geometry sandbox, and breadboard blowout station

Module 05 🔋

Class 5: Power, Energy & Batteries

Master electrical power equations, calculate energy usage and utility bills, explore battery chemical cells, and learn to prevent component heat burnout.

Syllabus Coverage
  • Electrical Power equations ($P = V \times I = I^2 \times R = V^2 / R$)
  • Electrical Energy & Time ($E = P \times t$, Joules vs. Watt-hours vs. Kilowatt-hours)
  • Utility Bill Math (kilowatt-hour usage and cost analysis for home appliances)
  • Voltaic Cell Chemistry: Anode, Cathode, Electrolyte, and chemical electron pump
  • Primary (disposable) vs. Secondary (rechargeable) battery cell types
  • Battery Configurations: Series voltage boost vs. Parallel capacity boost
  • Resistor Power Safety (under-rated component burnout and heat dissipation)
  • Interactive Voltaic Sandbox, Resistor Burnout, and Home Appliance Utility Billing Meter
