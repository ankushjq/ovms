Part 1: Resistors

Decoding Resistor Bands (4-Band & 5-Band)

Standard resistors use colored rings to encode their resistance value and tolerance limit. Being able to decode these quickly under time constraints is key.

The Calculation Rules

4-Band Resistor:
Value = (Band 1 Digit × 10 + Band 2 Digit) × Multiplier Ω ± Tolerance %

5-Band Resistor (Precision):
Value = (Band 1 × 100 + Band 2 × 10 + Band 3) × Multiplier Ω ± Tolerance %

Mnemonic Aid: "Bad Beers Rot Our Young Guts But Vodka Goes Well"
(Black=0, Brown=1, Red=2, Orange=3, Yellow=4, Green=5, Blue=6, Violet=7, Gray=8, White=9)

Part 2: Loops & Nodes

Kirchhoff's Laws (KCL & KVL)

Ohm's Law is sufficient for simple layouts, but solving compound grids requires Kirchhoff's two conservation laws:

Current Law (KCL)

Conservation of Charge: The total current entering any electrical junction (node) must exactly equal the total current leaving that node. No charge can accumulate.

$$\sum I_{in} = \sum I_{out} \implies I_1 = I_2 + I_3$$

Voltage Law (KVL)

Conservation of Energy: The algebraic sum of all potential differences (voltages) around any closed loop in a circuit must equal zero. Gains equal drops.

$$\sum V_{gains} - \sum V_{drops} = 0 \implies V_{battery} - V_{R1} - V_{R2} = 0$$

Part 3: Practical Lab

Lab Stations: Multimeters & Wheatstone Bridges

Science Olympiad lab stations heavily test how to physically hook up meters and analyze balanced resistance bridges.

Meter Placement Rules

Voltmeter (V): Must always connect in parallel across the component to measure the potential difference. Very high internal resistance.

Ammeter (A): Must connect in series (breaking the wire path). Must never connect in parallel (which creates a dead short circuit!). Very low resistance.

Ohmmeter (Ω): Component must be completely isolated from power (power source OFF or disconnected) to prevent meter damage.

The Wheatstone Bridge

A Wheatstone Bridge is a diamond network of 4 resistors used to determine an unknown resistance $R_x$.

When balanced, the galvanometer in the center reads 0V / 0A.

$$\text{Balanced condition:} \implies R_x = \frac{R_2}{R_1} \times R_3$$
