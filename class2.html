Part 2: Electromagnetism

Magnetism & The Birth of Electromagnetism

1. Permanent Magnetic Fields

Permanent magnets have poles (North & South). Field lines always travel from the North Pole to the South Pole externally. Identical poles repel; opposite poles attract.

2. Oersted's Discovery: Wire Magnetic Fields

In 1820, Hans Christian Ørsted noticed that a magnetic compass needle twitched when placed near a current-carrying wire. Moving charges generate magnetic fields! The field loops in concentric circles around the wire.

Circuit Protection: Fuses

A **fuse** contains a thin metal ribbon. If current is too high, it heats up and **melts**, opening the circuit to prevent a fire. Turn on appliances to exceed the **10A Fuse Limit**!
